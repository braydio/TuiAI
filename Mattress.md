# Mattress Types: Pros and Cons

## Innerspring
**Pros**
- Affordable
- Good airflow (cooler sleep)
- Bouncy, responsive feel

**Cons**
- Wears out faster
- Less contouring/support
- Can be noisy

---

## Memory Foam
**Pros**
- Excellent pressure relief
- Great motion isolation
- Conforms closely to body

**Cons**
- Retains heat (unless gel-infused)
- Less bounce
- Possible off-gassing

---

## Latex
**Pros**
- Durable and long-lasting
- Naturally cooler than memory foam
- Eco-friendly if natural latex

**Cons**
- Expensive
- Heavy and harder to move
- Limited firmness variety

---

## Hybrid
**Pros**
- Balanced support + comfort
- Good cooling from coil airflow
- Strong edge support

**Cons**
- Pricey
- Heavy/bulky
- Quality varies between models

---

## Pillow Top
**Pros**
- Plush, cushiony feel
- Adds comfort to a firm base
- Familiar hotel-like comfort

**Cons**
- Can sag or compress over time
- Traps more heat
- Harder to rotate/flip
